— 02 / Value Proposition

From seeing the world
to acting in the world.

Embodied intelligence requires more than seeing. It requires understanding how humans move, grasp, navigate, and interact with the physical world — all at once.

the same moment.

— 07 / Tasks

Humans don't just move.
They act.

From picking up a cup to cleaning an entire room — Human1000 captures the full spectrum of human behavior, not just isolated motions.

✋
01
Manipulation
Pick, place, open, close, pour, carry. Hands working with objects across every scale.
🚶
02
Navigation
Walk, turn, detour, approach, retreat. Bodies moving through space with intent.
🤝
03
Interaction
Person with object, person with environment, person with person.
🍽
04
Daily Activities
Eat, write, tidy, fetch, clean. The mundane choreography of human life.
